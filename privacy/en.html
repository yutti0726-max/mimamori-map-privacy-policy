Privacy Policy

Operator: 佐藤 雄一 (Yuichi Sato) | Contact: yutti0726@gmail.com | Effective date: 2026-10-22

"Imadoko Share" (the "App") lets you share your location, battery level and messages only with people you have approved, and only while you have sharing turned on. This policy explains what the App collects, why, where it is stored, how long it is kept, and how you can delete it.

2. Where information is stored, and third parties

  • Everything except the information that stays on your device is stored and processed by Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Messaging). Google acts as a service provider processing information on our instructions. Data location: Cloud Firestore: Tokyo (asia-northeast1).
  • Push notifications carry only the sender's display name and an internal connection identifier. Message text and location never pass through the push notification service.
  • To send a push notification, after you send a message the App tells a small relay program (it runs on Google Apps Script, a Google service) the message identifier, the connection identifier and your sign-in token. The relay checks them against the server and then asks Firebase Cloud Messaging to notify the other person. Message text and location are not sent to the relay.
  • To help prevent abuse, the App uses Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity. Google checks that a request comes from the genuine App on an unmodified Android device, and the App attaches the resulting verification token to its requests to Firebase. This involves Google receiving device-integrity information; we do not send your location or messages for this purpose.
  • Your location is obtained through Google Play services.
  • Maps are displayed with Google Maps.
  • Except for the providers above, we do not sell or provide your information to third parties. If we receive a legal request for disclosure, we respond in accordance with the law.
  • Communication is encrypted (TLS). Information from the server is kept only in the App's memory and is not saved to the device's storage. The App is excluded from device backups.

3. How your location is handled (important)

  • Your location is shared only with approved connections you have turned sharing on for, one person at a time. It needs both the other person's approval and your own consent.
  • Before asking for location permission (including "Allow all the time"), the App explains on screen that your location is used for sharing even when the App is closed.
  • While your location is being collected, a notification is always shown, and you can stop sharing from it with one tap. There is no hidden mode.
  • When you turn sharing off, the other person is told "Sharing off (turned off by them)". When you turn it off you can also remove your last location from their screen.
  • Sharing your location and recording your routes are separate switches that can be turned on and off independently.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Location, battery and sharing status: only the latest value is stored and it is overwritten with each update (no history is kept). It is deleted when you turn sharing off and remove your last location, when you end the connection, or when you delete your account. If the other person ends the connection, your last location is deleted the next time you open the App (it remains until then).
  • Messages: kept until you delete them. You can delete messages you sent at any time, and they disappear from the other person's screen too. When a connection ends, neither of you can read the messages any more. When you end a connection, the messages you sent and the message preview are deleted from the server (when your device is online), and all messages of that connection are also deleted from the server by a daily automatic clean-up after it ends.
  • Invite codes: valid for 10 minutes. A used code is deleted when the person who created it approves, declines or ends the connection. Other codes are deleted automatically.
  • Routes (on your device): you can keep them for 7 days, 30 days (default), 90 days or without limit, and delete them by day or all at once. Signing out deletes the routes saved on this device.
  • Deleting your account: in the App, "Settings" > "Delete account" deletes your profile, your sign-in account, your location, the messages you sent, your notification token and your block list, ends your connections, and removes your display name from them (the other person sees "Deleted user"). See the "Delete your account and data" page for details.
  • Information that may remain: messages you sent in a connection that has ended may stay on the server for a short time (unreadable by anyone) if your device was offline or the App was not opened. A daily automatic clean-up deletes them.

5. Your rights

  • You can check and correct your information (you can change your display name in the App) and ask for it to be deleted (see above). If you cannot do something in the App, contact us at yutti0726@gmail.com.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, such as access, portability or objection. Contact us and we will respond in accordance with the law that applies to you.
  • You can withdraw location permission at any time in your device settings. Doing so stops location sharing.

6. Children

The App is intended for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from younger children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us at yutti0726@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will tell you on this page and in the App.
