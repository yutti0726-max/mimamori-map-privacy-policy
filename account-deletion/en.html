Delete your account and data

App: Mimamori Map | Developer: 佐藤 雄一 (Yuichi Sato)

This page explains how to delete your Mimamori Map account and the data linked to it. You can do it yourself in the App, or ask us to do it if you cannot use the App.

1. Delete from inside the App

  1. Open the App and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation, enter your password and tap Delete.

The App then deletes your data as described below and returns to the sign-in screen.

2. What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Profile (display name) and sign-in account (email address, password)Deleted
Your last location, battery level and sharing statusDeleted
Messages you sent, and the message preview you last sent, in connections that were activeDeleted
Push notification token, and your list of blocked usersDeleted
Your connectionsEnded. Your display name is removed from them, and the other person sees "Deleted user"
Routes saved on your deviceDeleted

3. What may remain

  • Messages you sent in connections that had already ended or were still pending are also deleted from the server when you delete your account (when your device is online). Anything left behind is deleted by a daily automatic clean-up.
  • Invite codes you entered (a code contains the name of the person who created it) are removed by the person who created it, or expire.
  • Google keeps its own operational logs as part of providing Firebase, under its own retention rules. We cannot delete them.

4. If you cannot use the App

If the App is uninstalled, or you cannot sign in, email yutti0726@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account", and include your display name. Never send your password.

We will confirm that the request comes from the owner of the account, delete the data in section 2 within 30 days, and confirm by email.

5. Deleting only part of your data

  • A message: touch and hold a message you sent in the chat, then choose Delete.
  • Your routes: My routes > Manage data.
  • A connection: open the connection and choose Disconnect. Your last location and the messages you sent in it are deleted.
  • Your last location only: turn sharing off for that person and choose to remove your last location.

For how we handle your information in general, see the Privacy Policy.
